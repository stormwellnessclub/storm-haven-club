# Fire & Ice Body Wrap: research findings and next steps

## What the research found (checked Nov 22, 2026)

Short answer: plenty of spas offer **pieces** of this treatment. I found **no one** offering all of it as a single 90-minute ritual: cold legs and warm abdomen together, plus wood sculpting, lymphatic massage and a closing massage.

Similar treatments from other spas:
- **"Fire and Ice" names already exist**, but they're different treatments. Sweet Honey Spa ($180) uses a sauna dome, cold stones and a cryo facial. Aella Salon & Spa (another business with a name like your spa's) sells a "Fire & Ice Massage" with hot stones and cold, $129/$165. Using "Fire & Ice" is fine, but it isn't unique.
- **Hot and cold gel together:** Skinshape's "CryoThermic Body Treatment" (55 min, $95) uses one gel that does both, not cold on the legs and heat on the abdomen. Spa Virtue sells cryo gel and thermo gel as $25 add-ons to its wraps.
- **Legs and abdomen wrapped in one visit:** Buhala Spa in Vienna (50 min) does this, but with the same gel on both areas.
- **Wood sculpting with lymphatic massage** is common on its own, often 90 min for about $250 (Spa on 4th).
- **Lymphatic massage with a wrap:** Chicago Cryospa pairs a 90-minute lymphatic drainage massage with a magnesium wrap and infrared sauna.

**What makes yours different:** each area of the body gets its own treatment, cold for the legs and heat for the abdomen. That comes inside one ritual that also has exfoliation, wood sculpting, lymphatic massage and a closing massage. That combination is your selling point.
