- PT availability is decided only by server `pt_check_slot`/`pt_available_slots`; the UI only words its answer via `src/lib/ptAvailability.ts`, and overrides need an admin/manager reason. Why: one engine for staff booking, requests and future client booking.
- Spa single-treatment and series prices live in `spa_treatment_packages` (manager-editable in Spa Management → Packages) and are read through `src/lib/spaPackages.ts`; treatment copy stays in its own lib file without prices. Why: owner requires package prices editable by staff, never hard-coded.
- Zone-based spa services (Aella Sculpt) store one `treatment_zone_id` per appointment; a trigger copies the zone name onto the appointment and rejects inactive zones, and staff screens show it via `spaServiceLabel` in `spaZones.ts`. Why: zones stay staff-editable without separate services, and past bookings keep the zone they booked.
- Spa series purchases are server-only: `aella-sculpt-checkout` charges the saved card and saves `spa_client_packages` through `record_spa_client_package`; every series session books through `book_spa_package_session`, and a trigger enforces owner, expiration and sessions left (cancelled sessions don't count). Zones set to 'request' collect `spa_service_requests` instead of bookings, and `spa_services.public_menu = false` keeps a service off public lists. Why: clients must never create unpaid series or book areas the provider isn't offering yet.
