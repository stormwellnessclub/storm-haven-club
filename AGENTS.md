# Project Architecture Rules

- A frozen or past-due membership never blocks portal routing; benefits and facility access are enforced separately by status-aware UI and authoritative database functions.
- Paying a future membership-freeze fee records payment only; the date-driven freeze activation path pauses billing and changes membership status together on the scheduled Detroit date.- When a freeze ends, the dues subscription is unpaused with its billing anchor reset to that day (full charge, no proration); the freeze is only completed after Stripe confirms, otherwise billing_resume_error is set and the daily job retries. Why: members must never get unbilled days after a freeze.
- Saved-card charges pick the card in this order: the card the payer chose (verified to belong to their Stripe customer), then the customer's default payment method, then the newest card. Never charge whatever card Stripe lists first. Why: charging the newest card sent café and register charges to declined cards no one chose.
- A PT appointment covered by a package is always payment_status 'pass' (enforced by trigger), and past booked sessions held on a package are used automatically by an hourly job even if staff never check them out. Why: booked package sessions must always deduct and never show as unpaid.
- A cancelled membership never counts as membership for pricing or portal access: member-rate checks and `useUserMembership` ignore `status = 'cancelled'`, so those people use the non-member portal and prices while their history stays. Why: cancelled members must not buy at member rates.
- Campaign recipient lists page through every source with `.range()` loops and de-dupe by lowercased email; never rely on a single select. Why: single selects cap at 1,000 rows and silently dropped most contacts.
- Scheduled campaign emails use a one-time database timer per send (armed on schedule, removed after it fires or is cancelled), never a polling job. Why: no recurring cost when nothing is scheduled.
- Targeted texts use admin_person_activity() as the single per-person activity source; campaigns snapshot recipient user IDs plus phone-only imported contacts, and every send goes through send-sms without bypassConsent. send-sms accepts consent from the account, or by phone from non-member signup or imported contacts (phone_sms_consent), but an account opt-out always wins; STOP/START updates every source. Why: one source for segments, and carrier consent rules are never skipped.
- Revenue and sales-tax reports read only `revenue_ledger` (built by `sync-revenue-ledger` from Stripe balance transactions plus cash/check/Clover sales); never sum app tables like `manual_charges` or `cafe_orders` for revenue. Refunds are negative lines in the month issued, and tax, card fees and tips are kept separate from revenue. Why: per-report table sums double-counted register sales, missed dues, PT and cash, and changed closed months.
- Billing may only change a member's status between 'active' and 'past_due', always through `apply_billing_status_change`; frozen, cancelled, expired and pending-activation members are never changed by billing. Why: a frozen member's paused Stripe subscription still reports 'active', and activations stay a staff decision.
- Stripe is the source of truth for invoice status; `reconcile-billing` compares app billing records with Stripe nightly. It may only apply corrections that release a member (paid, voided, flag cleared, missed payment recorded); anything that would restrict a member goes to staff review. Why: lost webhooks left paid members flagged past due.
- When staff collect dues in club, the amount applied to the open Stripe invoice includes the processing fee. Why: subscription invoices carry a fee line; leaving it out kept the invoice open and Stripe charged the member again.
- Scheduled jobs authenticate to edge functions only with the vault-held internal task token: job commands call `public.internal_task_token()` and functions check it through `_shared/internalTaskToken.ts`. Never write a token, key or secret into a migration, job definition or source file. Payment-confirmation functions (`isServerCaller`) accept only the service-role key. Why: a plain-text token committed to the public repo let anyone call internal functions.
- Every marketing email (campaigns, promotions, presale blasts, Compose Email with data.marketing) goes through `_shared/marketingEmail.ts`: per-recipient never-expiring one-click unsubscribe (`email-unsubscribe`), club address footer, and one skip rule (unsubscribed, email opt-out, blocked). Transactional emails never use it. Why: unsubscribes used to go to the admin inbox and were never recorded.
- Membership dues are always collected by paying the member's open Stripe invoice itself (`charge-member-arrears`): card payments call `invoices.pay`, and cash/check payments mark that invoice paid outside Stripe. Never take dues as a separate charge while a bill is open. Why: a separate charge left the bill open and Stripe collected the same month again.
- A bill's dates come from its subscription line periods (`_shared/invoicePeriod.ts`), never from `invoice.period_start/end`, which hold the previous period. Why: bills were labelled one month early.
- Payment failures and successes for the same invoice must link regardless of arrival order: database rules resolve a failure recorded after its success, keep paid months paid unless deliberately reopened (`reopened_at`), and keep reminders recovered; the webhook re-checks the invoice in Stripe before acting on a failure. Why: late failure notices re-flagged members who had paid.
- A standalone dues charge is applied to an open bill automatically only on an exact amount match to a bill that existed at (or within a week after) the payment and only once (Stripe metadata `applied_to_invoice`); everything else is staff review. Why: applying a payment to the wrong bill silently forgives real debt.
- `payment_attempts` holds bill (Stripe invoice) payments only, each with its `stripe_invoice_id`; register, café, shop and spa charges live in `manual_charges` and the revenue ledger. Jobs that copy Stripe charges must skip charges without an invoice and must never match members by email. Why: the nightly rebuild copied every charge without its bill, so failures never linked to the later payment and café sales were counted twice.
