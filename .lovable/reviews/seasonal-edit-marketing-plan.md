# Seasonal Edit: what the numbers say, and a better final week

## 1. What actually happened (your own numbers)

| Who got it | Delivered | Bounced | Bought within 7 days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Members (122) | 99% | under 1% | 8 people on email 1, 6 on email 2 |
| Non-members with an account (~740) | 97% | about 3% | 3 people |
| Imported email list, no account (~2,530) | 91% | **8.6%** | **0 people, across 3 emails** |

- A healthy gym list bounces about 1% ([source](https://emailcalculator.com/benchmarks/fitness-gyms)). Your imported list bounces 8 times that. Gmail and Yahoo read that as a sender who doesn't know their list, so they start filtering everything you send, members included ([Google](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126?hl=en), [Yahoo](https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/)).
- Most of the message was inside pictures, there was no plain-text version, and the same people got 3 big emails in 4 days.
- Most real buyers were **members** and people who already had an account. The cold list produced nothing.

## 3. What the research says works

- **Fewer, more personal emails beat polished flyers.** In published tests, plain, personal emails got 30% to 3× more clicks than designed ones ([HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/plain-text-vs-html-emails-data), [NextAfter](https://www.nextafter.com/experiments/how-more-personal-email-content-affects-clickthrough-rate-2/), [Threadpoint](https://www.threadpoint.agency/blogs/learn-e-mail-marketing/plain-text-triumph-how-a-personal-touch-beat-a-designed-newsletter-for-brickhouse-nutrition)).
- **Interactive "choose or reveal" emails can work much better than flat offers.** In one holiday scratch-off email, clicks rose almost 4× and sales over 6× ([AT&T](https://iacaward.org/iac/winner/16713/bkv--att-wins-2017-iac-award-for-building-holiday-engagement-from-scratch-atts-digital-scratch-off-campaign.html), [Kitsch](https://www.spellbound.io/blog/kitsch-interactive-labor-day-game-email-generates-4x-revenue-than-other-campaigns)). These are big brands, so expect a smaller lift for us.
- **Your best customers react to early access and perks, not just discounts.** Repeat buyers drove the 2025 Black Friday growth ([Klaviyo](https://www.klaviyo.com/newsroom/bfcm-holiday-shopping)).
- **The final days sell the most.** Cyber Sunday and Cyber Monday grew fastest ([Klaviyo](https://www.klaviyo.com/newsroom/bfcm-holiday-shopping)). The usual pattern is a light touch on Thanksgiving, a few sends from Friday to Sunday, then a last-day push ([Gosh Digital](https://www.goshdigital.co/blog/klaviyo-bfcm-email-strategy)).
- **Opens are only a rough guide.** Many phones open emails automatically, so judge campaigns on clicks and purchases ([Ground Standard](https://www.groundstandard.com/unlock-higher-retention-essential-email-campaigns-for-fitness-studios)).

## 4. Four ideas for Nov 24–30 (pick any)

**A. "What do you want December to feel like?" (interactive pick)**
The email asks one question and shows three tappable answers: *Stronger* / *Rested* / *Someone else's turn*. Each answer opens the one offer that fits: training, massage, or a gift. We see who tapped what, and the next text only goes to people who showed interest, with the offer they picked.
Subject idea: "One question before December"

**B. The reveal card (interactive gift)**
Your tissue-card animation becomes a gift finder. "Who is on your list?" Tap *Her* / *Him* / *Someone who never stops* / *Yourself*, and the card opens to the right massage or class package. This is for gift-givers before the holidays.
Subject idea: "For the person who never books it for themselves"

**C. A short note, not a flyer (personal)**
A plain email of 4–6 lines from one of your trainers, signed by name. It says what consistent semi-private training changes over 8 weeks, with one link and no pictures. These usually reach the main inbox and get the most replies. (Your brand rules avoid owner first-person, so this comes from a trainer or "the team".)
Subject idea: "What 30 sessions actually changes"

**D. Members first (early access)**
Members get the final-week offer 24 hours before everyone else, plus a gift-for-a-friend option at the regular price. It rewards the group that is already buying.
Subject idea: "Members, a day early"
