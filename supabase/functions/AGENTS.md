# Edge function billing & email rules

- Saved-card charges pick the card in this order: the card the payer chose (verified to belong to their Stripe customer), then the customer's default payment method, then the newest card. Never charge whatever card Stripe lists first. Why: charging the newest card sent café and register charges to declined cards no one chose.
- Stripe is the source of truth for invoice status; `reconcile-billing` compares app billing records with Stripe nightly. It may only apply corrections that release a member (paid, voided, flag cleared, missed payment recorded); anything that would restrict a member goes to staff review. Why: lost webhooks left paid members flagged past due.
- When staff collect dues in club, the amount applied to the open Stripe invoice includes the processing fee. Why: subscription invoices carry a fee line; leaving it out kept the invoice open and Stripe charged the member again.
- Every marketing email (campaigns, promotions, presale blasts, Compose Email with data.marketing) goes through `_shared/marketingEmail.ts`: per-recipient never-expiring one-click unsubscribe (`email-unsubscribe`), club address footer, and one skip rule (unsubscribed, email opt-out, blocked). Transactional emails never use it. Why: unsubscribes used to go to the admin inbox and were never recorded.
- Membership dues are always collected by paying the member's open Stripe invoice itself (`charge-member-arrears`): card payments call `invoices.pay`, and cash/check payments mark that invoice paid outside Stripe. Never take dues as a separate charge while a bill is open. Why: a separate charge left the bill open and Stripe collected the same month again.
- A bill's dates come from its subscription line periods (`_shared/invoicePeriod.ts`), never from `invoice.period_start/end`, which hold the previous period. Why: bills were labelled one month early.
- Payment failures and successes for the same invoice must link regardless of arrival order: database rules resolve a failure recorded after its success, keep paid months paid unless deliberately reopened (`reopened_at`), and keep reminders recovered; the webhook re-checks the invoice in Stripe before acting on a failure. Why: late failure notices re-flagged members who had paid.
- A standalone dues charge is applied to an open bill automatically only on an exact amount match to a bill that existed at (or within a week after) the payment and only once (Stripe metadata `applied_to_invoice`); everything else is staff review. Why: applying a payment to the wrong bill silently forgives real debt.
- `payment_attempts` holds bill (Stripe invoice) payments only, each with its `stripe_invoice_id`; register, café, shop and spa charges live in `manual_charges` and the revenue ledger. Jobs that copy Stripe charges must skip charges without an invoice and must never match members by email. Why: the nightly rebuild copied every charge without its bill, so failures never linked to the later payment and café sales were counted twice.
- Client-side card charges send a per-attempt `idempotencyKey` and lock the button from the first tap; a charge whose booking then fails is auto-refunded via `refund_own_unbooked_charge`. Why: slow charges let repeat taps bill clients several times.
