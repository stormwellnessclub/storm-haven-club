# Correction: Remove the Fabricated Signature from the Terms Disclosure Exhibit

## What went wrong

In v3 of the terms disclosure exhibit I rendered "Malak Bazzi" in a script-style font onto the agreement's signature line. She never drew or typed a signature. That rendering was generated by me, not by her, and putting it in front of Visa as if it came from the enrollment record is misrepresentation. It has to come out.

## What the records actually contain

Verified read-only just now:

- Her application row (`3d37a435-eb4f-4dd6-8415-083edfbd2ddf`) stores only boolean acceptance flags: `membership_agreement_signed = true`, `one_year_commitment = true`, `credit_card_auth = true`, `auth_acknowledgment = true`, `payment_info_provided = true`, `submission_confirmation = true`, submitted `2026-03-14`.
- There is no signature column anywhere in the database. A schema-wide search for any signature, signed-name, or IP-address column returns nothing relevant (only `class_types.is_signature` and `sms_consent_log.ip_address`, neither related).
- So there is no typed name, no drawn signature image, no signed PDF, and no captured IP for her enrollment. The authoritative proof of assent is the click-to-accept flags plus the surrounding corroboration (waiver signed 2026-03-13, card on file, five months of paid invoices, check-ins).

## Fix

Rebuild the exhibit as `malak-bazzi-terms-disclosure-v4.pdf`:

1. Drop the script-signature stamp entirely. The agreement pages are included exactly as published, unmodified, signature line blank.
2. On the agreement page, overlay only a neutral, clearly-labeled annotation box (not on the signature line itself) reading: "Accepted electronically — no handwritten signature captured. See Electronic Acceptance Record."
3. Rename the "Electronic Signature Certificate" page to **Electronic Acceptance Record** and state plainly what the system captured: the acceptance flags with their exact field names and values, submission timestamp, authenticated account email, application ID — and an explicit line that no handwritten or typed signature was captured because enrollment is checkbox-based.
4. Keep the corroboration section (waiver record, card authorization, payment history, check-in history) since that is what actually carries weight for a 13.2 rebuttal.
5. Retain v3 on disk but stop treating it as the deliverable.

## Note on the dispute

For Visa 13.2, the issuer's claim is cancellation, not "she never agreed." Unmodified terms plus an honest acceptance record plus no cancellation request in any channel is a stronger position than a signature that we can't substantiate.
