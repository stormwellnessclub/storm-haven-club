# Edge function billing & email rules

- One-time Checkout Sessions in `stripe-payment` use `labelCheckoutPurchases` to copy actual catalog or inline item names onto the PaymentIntent, preserving explicit descriptions and all payment options. Why: named Checkout line items alone leave Stripe's main payment description blank.

- Saved-card charges pick the card in this order: the card the payer chose (verified to belong to their Stripe customer), then the customer's default payment method, then the newest card. Never charge whatever card Stripe lists first. Why: charging the newest card sent café and register charges to declined cards no one chose.
- Stripe is the source of truth for invoice status; `reconcile-billing` compares app billing records with Stripe nightly. It may only apply corrections that release a member (paid, voided, flag cleared, missed payment recorded); anything that would restrict a member goes to staff review. Why: lost webhooks left paid members flagged past due.
- When staff collect dues in club, the amount applied to the open Stripe invoice includes the processing fee. Why: subscription invoices carry a fee line; leaving it out kept the invoice open and Stripe charged the member again.
- Every marketing email (campaigns, promotions, presale blasts, Compose Email with data.marketing) goes through `_shared/marketingEmail.ts`: per-recipient never-expiring one-click unsubscribe (`email-unsubscribe`), club address footer, and one skip rule (unsubscribed, email opt-out, blocked). Transactional emails never use it. Why: unsubscribes used to go to the admin inbox and were never recorded.
- Legacy invitation and announcement blasts use `sendMarketingEmail` to enforce suppression and system unsubscribe headers at the send boundary; consent lookup errors stop sends. Why: older direct-provider sends bypassed the shared marketing protections.
- Membership dues are always collected by paying the member's open Stripe invoice itself (`charge-member-arrears`): card payments call `invoices.pay`, and cash/check payments mark that invoice paid outside Stripe. Never take dues as a separate charge while a bill is open. Why: a separate charge left the bill open and Stripe collected the same month again.
- A bill's dates come from its subscription line periods (`_shared/invoicePeriod.ts`), never from `invoice.period_start/end`, which hold the previous period. Why: bills were labelled one month early.
- Payment failures and successes for the same invoice must link regardless of arrival order: database rules resolve a failure recorded after its success, keep paid months paid unless deliberately reopened (`reopened_at`), and keep reminders recovered; the webhook re-checks the invoice in Stripe before acting on a failure. Why: late failure notices re-flagged members who had paid.
- A standalone dues charge is applied to an open bill automatically only on an exact amount match to a bill that existed at (or within a week after) the payment and only once (Stripe metadata `applied_to_invoice`); everything else is staff review. Why: applying a payment to the wrong bill silently forgives real debt.
- `payment_attempts` holds bill (Stripe invoice) payments only, each with its `stripe_invoice_id`; register, café, shop and spa charges live in `manual_charges` and the revenue ledger. Jobs that copy Stripe charges must skip charges without an invoice and must never match members by email. Why: the nightly rebuild copied every charge without its bill, so failures never linked to the later payment and café sales were counted twice.
- Client-side card charges send a per-attempt `idempotencyKey` and lock the button from the first tap; a charge whose booking then fails is auto-refunded via `refund_own_unbooked_charge`. Why: slow charges let repeat taps bill clients several times.
- Time-limited PT sale packages are `pt_packs` rows with `promo_key`, `promo_audience` (member/non_member) and sale start/end; `pt-promo-checkout` charges the saved card and creates the package through the staff sale-intent path, rejecting the wrong audience or an ended window server-side. Why: sale prices must stop on time and members/non-members can't buy each other's price.
- PT payment plans with `session_release` (one number per payment) hold sessions until each installment is paid (`pt_plan_apply_session_hold` + paid trigger); plans with `session_release` NULL (all Black Friday plans) give the full package at sale and staff watch "used vs paid for" on the client AutoPay tab. Online plan buys go through `pt-promo-checkout` → `admin-create-pt-payment-plan` (service key). Use-by dates never come from plan length. Why: the owner gives plan buyers the full package and tracks usage against payments.
- Seasonal Edit class packages are `seasonal_class_offers` rows (price, classes, expiry days, audience any/non_member/member, first-time rule, day pass, guest class credits, café credit, on/off and dates); `seasonal-class-checkout` checks eligibility with `seasonal_class_offer_block_reason`, and the webhook's `seasonal_class_pack` branch issues each item once per payment intent: one `class_passes` row, a linked `guest_passes` day pass, a `seasonal_guest_class_credits` row (redeemed/cancelled only via its RPCs), and an expiring `cafe_credit_ledger` 'seasonal_grant' (unspent expired credit is excluded from balance and settled before redemption). Regular `class_pricing` is never touched. Why: promo packages must not change regular prices, and nothing may be issued or redeemed twice.
- Gift class packs are claimed only with the private code from the gift email (`gift_pass_claim_tokens`, server-only); café prices change only by manager+ (price trigger); clients never insert class passes. Why: unverified sign-ups and front desk could otherwise take gifts, discount sales or mint passes.
- Campaign Studio sends in time-limited rounds that hand off to the next round via the internal token, skipping anyone already handled (sent, failed or blocked); "unsubscribe" replies reaching `receive-email` are recorded as opt-outs, not inbox messages. Why: large lists outlast one function run, and opt-outs must not fill the admin inbox.
- Scheduled jobs authenticate to edge functions only with the vault-held internal task token: job commands call `public.internal_task_token()` and functions check it through `_shared/internalTaskToken.ts`. Never write a token, key or secret into a migration, job definition or source file. Payment-confirmation functions (`isServerCaller`) accept only the service-role key. Why: a plain-text token committed to the public repo let anyone call internal functions.
- Spa reminder timing converts UTC once to Detroit fields via send-spa-reminders/timing.ts. Why: double conversion sent reminders after visits.
- Guest spa checkout accepts any public anon key; account tokens need verified auth. Why: rotated keys must not break guests.
- Staff PT sales may change price/sessions for one sale only (manager+, required reason, stored as sale-intent overrides; catalog untouched). Any package can use a custom monthly plan with no preset template (`customPlan` in `admin-create-pt-payment-plan`, schedule from `pt_build_custom_plan_schedule` using the sale's own price); a changed price can never use a preset plan. Why: preset amounts can't match a discounted total, and many packages have no preset plans.
- Massage gifts are `gift_card_offers` rows (price, covered spa service IDs, on/off, sale dates); `create-gift-card-checkout` takes price, name and covered services only from the offer while it's on, and the card stores `covers_service_ids`. In online spa booking an unused massage gift covers its listed treatment in full at its normal length (`giftCardCoverage` + `reserve_spa_guest_checkout`), using up the card's actual value; any other use is plain dollar credit. Desk checkout applies codes only through `staff_apply_spa_gift_card` (same rule, staff roles only, locks card + appointment). Why: a $120 massage gift must pay for a $155 massage without the recipient owing the difference.
